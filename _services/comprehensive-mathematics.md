summary: Many students approach mathematics with uncertainty, often viewing it as a collection of formulas and procedures disconnected from real life. In reality, mathematics plays a fundamental role in describing patterns, relationships, and structures that shape the world around us. Comprehensive Mathematics seeks to bridge this gap by presenting mathematics as a meaningful, practical, and interconnected discipline. The course covers selected topics in algebra, analytic geometry, trigonometry, and discrete mathematics, emphasizing understanding, visualization, and problem solving. Rather than treating these topics as isolated ideas, the course highlights how they work together to explain relationships and support logical reasoning. Through this approach, students are expected to strengthen their foundational skills and develop a deeper appreciation of mathematics as a powerful tool for analyzing and solving real-world problems.
