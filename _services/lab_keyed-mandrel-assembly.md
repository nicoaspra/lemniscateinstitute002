summary: Machining is best learned through direct experience, where understanding develops from the careful execution of each operation and the evaluation of results. The fabrication of a keyed mandrel assembly provides an ideal context for developing these essential skills, as it integrates multiple machining processes into a single, meaningful project. This laboratory workbook guides students through a sequence of machining activities involved in producing a keyed mandrel assembly, including turning, drilling, boring, threading, keyway cutting, and fitting operations. Each activity emphasizes proper machine setup, tool selection, measurement, and adherence to specified dimensions. By progressing from individual operations to final assembly, students gain a clearer understanding of how accuracy, alignment, and workmanship affect the performance of a mechanical component. Emphasis is placed on developing safe work practices, precision in measurement, and problem-solving skills during actual shop work. Through this hands-on approach, students are expected to strengthen their technical competence and appreciation for the discipline required in machining, while recognizing the importance of process planning and quality in manufacturing.
