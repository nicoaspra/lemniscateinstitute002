# Objective
This activity aims to introduce students to the process of keyway cutting using a shaper machine. Students will learn how to align, secure, and machine a straight internal keyway on the previously fabricated flange. The focus will be on proper workholding, tool setup, and accurate stroke adjustment.

# Materials and Equipment
The following materials and tools are essential for successfully completing this activity. Ensure they are available, complete, and in proper working condition before starting.
- Finished Flange with Bore
- Shaper Machine
- Shaping Tool
- Vise
- Vernier Caliper
- Safety Goggles


# Procedure
## Workpiece Preparation and Setup
<ol>
    <li>Ensure that the <strong>flange</strong> has been bored to the final diameter and that its internal bore is clean and free from burrs.</li>
    <li>Mount the flange securely in the <strong>vise</strong> on the shaper table, ensuring that the bore is oriented vertically and that the workpiece is firmly clamped.</li>
    <li>Use parallels or blocks if needed to raise the workpiece and ensure the bore is aligned with the tool stroke path.</li>
    <li>Visually confirm that the flange bore is square with respect to the shaper ram's travel.</li>
</ol>

## Tool Setup and Stroke Adjustment
<ol start="5">
    <li>Mount the <strong>internal shaping tool</strong> to the tool holder of the shaper.</li>
    <li>Adjust the stroke length to slightly exceed the depth of the bore.</li>
    <li>Position the tool to align with the centerline of the bore for keyway placement.</li>
    <li>Use manual jog or slow power strokes to check for tool clearance and alignment before beginning full-speed shaping.</li>
</ol>

## Keyway Cutting
<ol start="9">
    <li>Begin shaping using light passes. After each stroke, advance the tool slightly using the vertical feed or cross-slide feed.</li>
    <li>Periodically retract and clear chips from the bore to avoid tool jamming.</li>
    <li>Continue shaping until the desired <strong>keyway depth and width</strong> are achieved, based on the specified dimensions.</li>
    <li>Measure the keyway dimensions using a <strong>vernier caliper</strong> or depth gauge.</li>
</ol>

## Finishing and Inspection
<ol start="13">
    <li>Deburr the edges of the keyway to remove sharp corners or raised metal.</li>
    <li>Clean the workpiece and remove it from the vise.</li>
    <li>Inspect the keyway for <strong>dimensional accuracy</strong> and <strong>surface finish</strong>.</li>
    <li>Test-fit the intended key into the slot to ensure a proper fit.</li>
</ol>
